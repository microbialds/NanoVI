Flagged species

A species is flagged in a sample when its shared_read_fraction is at least {{ f.cutoff }} (most of its expected reads are split with another reported species) or when it shares an identical 16S with other species of its genus (identical_16s_species). {{ f.n_flagged }} species are flagged in at least one sample: {{ f.n_shared }} by shared_read_fraction and {{ f.n_identical }} by an identical 16S ({{ m.figref(s, "flagged_species") }}, {{ m.tabref(s, "flagged") }}). Their abundance rests on reads that 16S does not assign to them alone.
